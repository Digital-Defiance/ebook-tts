# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback,
protect other people's private data and copyrighted material, and avoid abusive,
discriminatory, threatening, or sexualized conduct.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation
that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository host's maintainer-contact or abuse-reporting facilities. Maintainers
will protect reporter privacy to the extent practical and respond proportionally
to the context and severity.
